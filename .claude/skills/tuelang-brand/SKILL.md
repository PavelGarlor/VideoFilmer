# Tuelang brand

Tuelang teaches German online to adults in **small live cohorts** (max 10 students), with real teachers and an **optional trip to Berlin**. Website: **tuelang.com**. Machine-readable values live in `brand.json` next to this file.

## Colours

| Role | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Ink | `#1C1C1E` | Main text, dark scenes, buttons |
| Orange | `#F4A227` | The wordmark dot, highlights, prices, CTAs on dark |
| Teal | `#1A7A7A` | Secondary scenes, accents, links |
| Lime | `#95ED68` | Course-page hero, bold attention scenes (with ink text) |
| Deep green | `#0F2B29` | Rich dark backgrounds |
| Paper | `#F4F4F6` | Light scenes |
| Grey | `#6B7280` | Secondary text |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Text on ink/teal/deep green, cards |

Pairings that work: ink text on lime, white on ink with orange highlights, white on teal, ink on paper with white cards. Avoid orange text on lime or white (contrast is too low).

## Type

- **Plus Jakarta Sans**: headings, extra-bold, tight tracking (−0.03em). The wordmark is set in it.
- **DM Sans**: body text.
- **Fraunces** (serif, often italic): small human touches, such as the asides under German phrases ("— your first sentence").
- All three are free Google Fonts. In native Swift renders where they aren't installed, use the system font (SF Pro), heavy/semibold.

## Wordmark

"**Tuelang**" in Plus Jakarta Sans extra-bold, tracking −0.03em, followed by a small **orange (`#F4A227`) dot** sitting slightly below the x-height middle. There is no separate logo image; always draw the wordmark as text + dot. Ink on light backgrounds, white on dark ones.

## Voice

- Warm, direct, a little playful. Talk like a good teacher, not an ad.
- Concrete over hype: "order coffee in Berlin", not "master German fast".
- Honest: a beginner course gets you to **A1**, so never promise fluency.
- British spelling: practise, memorisation, colour.
- German phrases as flavour, always understandable from context:
  - "Einen Kaffee, bitte." (actually useful)
  - "Ich heiße…" (your first sentence)
  - "Gut, aber…" (the correction that sticks)
  - "Wieder und wieder." (until it clicks)
- Signature ideas: speak from day one · real feedback, not algorithms · vocabulary that fits your life · small group, same faces · Berlin together.

## Don'ts

- Don't invent testimonials, numbers or results.
- Don't show "X of 10 seats left" unless you've just checked the live page.
- Don't use other brands' logos or music without a licence.
- Don't put phone numbers, emails or personal accounts in videos or captions. Point people to **tuelang.com** only.
